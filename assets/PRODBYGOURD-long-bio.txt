Breaking through the noise of life, PRODBYGOURD delivers a maximalist distraction for those seeking one through his electronic tracks. Though influenced by the likes of Ninajirachi and Daft Punk, PRODBYGOURD produces a wide range of genres for his uniquely clients.

Hailing from Boston, Massachusetts, PRODBYGOURD started to develop his musical abilities at a young age. Infatuated with music contained in the 2 Animusic DVDs his father gave to him, PRODBYGOURD started to create musical tracks on his family computer using Apple’s GarageBand and continued to develop his technical abilities in high school. Throughout his time in the Honors college at Belmont University, GOURD developed his musical prowess mastering classical voice, music theory, composition, orchestration, and arranging. Along the way, he also developed skills in engineering, sound design, the recording arts, landing him a Stage-Hand position at Gnome Recording Studios in Nashville, TN.

Offering freelance services since 2023, PRODBYGOURD has produced a wide range of tracks. Most notably, in 2024, he arranged and produced “natural light” with the indie pop artist, Vicky Gao. PRODBYGOURD is “…[a]lways a joy to work with. He always sees my vision clearly and executes it precisely,” said Vicky Gao at the April release party.

PRODBYGOURD is currently working on his first studio EP entitled “Club Potential.” Throughout his life, GOURD at times felt as if his days were blending together. Life felt mundane and boring. This EP is an in-your-face, loud, and eccentric release from this boredom.
